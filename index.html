About

Orbit is a webplayable interactive story about 3 rovers and a lander:

Opportunity & Spirit, twins who explored Mars.

Apollo 15's Rover and its lander Falcon, who explored the Moon.

Pick a world, play a story as one of its two heroes, then take the Test to see how much you remember. Every fact and picture comes from real NASA missions.

The missions

Spirit and Opportunity were built for a mission of about three months. Opportunity kept driving for more than 14 years. On the Moon, Apollo 15's rover carried two astronauts 27.8 km across the Hadley-Apennine region, while its lander Falcon left a science station behind.

How to play

  • Click, or press Space or Enter, to move the story forward. Keys 1-4 pick an answer.
  • Every finished chapter writes a page in your book. Open it with the book button.
  • Finish a story to unlock its Test, then try to beat your best score.
  • Esc goes back (inside a story it opens the menu). Sound and progress are in Settings.

Spirit & Opportunity

Two robots. One planet. A 90-day mission nobody expected them to outlive.

In January 2004, twin rovers named Spirit and Opportunity landed on opposite sides of Mars with one job: find out if the Red Planet was ever wet. They were built to last three months. Opportunity kept rolling for almost fifteen years.

Along the way came dust storms, wheels stuck in sand, and very long, very cold nights. Think you can keep two tin cans alive on a planet that really doesn't want them there?

Press Play Story to find out.

Apollo 15

One folding car. One very dusty Moon.

In July 1971, Apollo 15 landed at the foot of the Apennine mountains, near Hadley Rille. The Lunar Roving Vehicle rode there folded up in a bay on the side of the lunar module Falcon. Once unfolded, it let astronauts David Scott and James Irwin drive far beyond where their legs could ever take them.

Play as Rover or as Falcon and see the mission from two points of view: three drives, three moonwalks, a science station, and a hammer and a feather.

Press Play Story to find out.
